2. About the Game

Melto Run is a 2D online multiplayer platformer developed and published by TST Labs.

The Game includes features such as:

  • Friendly Race, allowing players to compete on selected levels.
  • 1v1 Duel against friends or AI opponents.
  • Shooting Mode against friends or AI opponents.
  • Survival Sprint, where players attempt to survive while being chased by a zombie.
  • More than 20 playable characters with different abilities.
  • Thousands of platforming levels.
  • Dynamic gameplay events, including zero gravity, reversed gravity, lava surfaces, elastic surfaces and slippery ice.
  • Online multiplayer functionality and other gameplay features.
